# Help & support

Something not working, or a spot that's wrong? Email [support@mesamesa.app](mailto:support@mesamesa.app) and we'll get back to you. It helps to say what phone you use and what you were doing when it happened.

Looking for what Mesa does with your data? That's in the [privacy policy](/privacy/). The rules for using Mesa are in the [terms of service](/terms/).

## Getting started

### Do I need an account?

Yes. Everything Mesa shows is ranked for your taste, and your saved spots, crawls and stats live in your account. Sign up with email, or use Apple or Google.

### Why is Mesa asking for an invite code?

Mesa is invite-only for now while we open it up a few people at a time. You can still create an account; it waits on the waitlist until you enter a code. If someone sent you one, type it on that screen. Dashes and capitals don't matter. No code yet? Your account is saved, so sign in again once you have one. You can also sign out or delete the account from the same screen.

### I didn't get the confirmation or password email

Check spam first. Then try again from the sign-in screen: "Forgot password?" sends a new reset link, and signing in with an unconfirmed email offers to resend the confirmation. Open the link on the phone that has Mesa and tap "Open Mesa" on the page that appears. Opened a confirmation link on a computer? Your email is still confirmed, so just sign in on your phone.

### Mesa can't find my location

Mesa asks for location only while you use the app. If you said no, open your phone's Settings → Mesa → Location and choose "While Using the App". Or skip it: tap the location pill at the top of Explore and pick a city by hand.

### Can I look at another city?

Yes. Tap the location pill at the top of Explore, then search for a neighborhood or city or pick one from the list. Tap "Use my current location" to come back.

## Your picks

### How do I change what Mesa suggests?

Tap the round sliders button at the top of Explore, or Profile → My taste. Set your vibes, budget, cuisines, allergies and diet, and how far you'll walk or drive. You can add your own words too, like "rooftop" or "dim sum". Your picks update right away.

### What does the match % mean?

It's how well a spot fits what you told Mesa: your vibes, budget, cravings and how far you'll go, plus the mood you picked if you picked one. Tap the badge on any spot to see it explained. No taste set yet means no match %.

### What do the badges mean?

- **Michelin-Recognized**: listed in the Michelin Guide, with a star, a Bib Gourmand or a recommendation.
- **Buzzworthy**: featured by food writers. The place page links to the coverage under "As seen in".
- **Local Favorite**: rated 4.5 stars or higher by 500 or more reviewers.
- **Hidden Gem**: rated 4.6 stars or higher, with fewer than 150 reviews so far.

### How does search work?

Type a craving, a dish or a place's name. Places whose name matches come first, then the rest in order of fit. Search by name and that place leads the list even if it's outside your usual budget.

### How do I sort or filter?

Under the search bar: "Open now", a mood (date night, quick bite, brunch and more), and the kind of place. The sort control on the right switches between best match, top rated and nearest.

### Why don't I see chains?

Mesa is for finding local spots, so big chains stay out of your picks. Search for one by name and it shows up.

### What is "AI picks", and can I turn it off?

Mesa asks once, after you sign in, whether to turn on "Picks made for you". That's AI picks, and it stays off unless you say yes. With it on, Mesa sends your taste preferences (including any allergies and diet), a summary of what you save, and your searches and crawl requests to Anthropic's Claude to rank spots for you and plan crawls. Your name, email, photo and GPS coordinates are never sent.

Tapped "Not now", or changed your mind? Switch it on or off anytime in Profile → Settings → AI picks. With it off, Mesa ranks with its own scoring. The [privacy policy](/privacy/#ai-picks) lists exactly what is shared.

## Places

### A place is closed, moved or has wrong hours

Place details come from Google Maps and can lag behind real life. Email us the name of the spot and what's wrong. Holiday hours aren't shown yet, so check with the place before you go.

### What does "Reported closed" mean?

A news article says the place has shut, but its Google listing hasn't caught up. Mesa shows who reported it and stops suggesting the place. If that's wrong, tell us.

### Where do "Crowd favorites" and the review summary come from?

From the place's public reviews. Mesa reads them and pulls out the dishes people keep mentioning and a short summary of what they say. They aren't Mesa's own opinions, and a menu can change.

### Can Mesa tell me if a dish is safe for my allergy?

No. Mesa steers suggestions away from obvious conflicts, but it can't guarantee any place or dish is allergen-free. Always confirm with the restaurant.

## Wildcard

### What is Wildcard?

A deck of good spots that are open right now, for when you can't decide. Tap the round card button beside the bottom bar, pick what you're in the mood for, then swipe right to save a spot or left to pass. Tap a card for the full story.

### I swiped the wrong way

Tap the undo button under the deck to bring back the last card. Spots you pass on stay out of the way for a while; they aren't gone for good.

## Crawls

### How do I plan a crawl?

Open the Crawl tab. Pick a theme, add a vibe or two if you like, choose walking or driving, how far and how many stops (two to four), then tap "Plan my crawl". Use "Anything else?" for requests like "end with dessert".

### Where did my crawl go?

A crawl you plan but don't save is kept for 7 days. Save it and it stays on your profile under Crawls.

### What does finishing a crawl do?

Tap "We crushed this crawl" when you're done. It's added to your stats, along with the distance if you walked it.

### Can I share a crawl?

Open the crawl and tap the share button at the top. Pick Friends or Anyone with the link, then "Send the link". Other people see the stops, route and times. They don't see where you started unless you turn on "Show where it starts".

### Someone shared a crawl with me. What can I do with it?

Save it to keep it on your profile, tap "I'm in" to let them know you're coming, or "Copy to my crawls" to get your own copy. The copy is yours alone and they won't see it.

## Friends

### How do I add a friend?

Profile → Friends. Type their handle and send a request; you're friends once they accept. If you invite someone to Mesa with one of your invite codes, you're friends as soon as they use it.

### Where are my invite codes?

Profile → Friends → Your invite codes, while Mesa is invite-only. Each code lets one person in. Tap "Send" to share one.

### What can my friends see?

Your name, handle and photo, plus any crawl or list you set to Friends or Anyone with the link. Nothing else: not your saved spots, your taste, your allergies or where you are.

### How do I remove a friend?

Profile → Friends, tap the person, then "Remove friend". They aren't told.

### How do I block someone?

Open their page (tap their name anywhere you see it) and tap "Block". They can't find you, send you requests or see anything you share, and you won't see theirs. They aren't told. To undo it: Settings → Blocked people.

### How do I report someone or something?

Tap "Report" on the person's page, or on the crawl or list. Pick a reason and add anything we should know. We look at every report within 24 hours, and the other person isn't told it was you. You can also email [support@mesamesa.app](mailto:support@mesamesa.app).

## Lists

### What's a list?

A named group of places, like "Date night" or "Birthday dinner options". Make one in Profile → Lists, or from any place: tap the list button at the top of its page.

### Can friends add to my list?

Yes. Open the list, tap "Who can edit" and add friends. They can add and remove places; only you can rename, share or delete the list. The list shows who added each place.

### Who can see my list?

Only you and the friends you added, until you share it. Open the list, tap the share button and pick Friends or Anyone with the link.

### I opened a shared link and it says it isn't available

The owner made it private or deleted it, or it's shared with friends only and you aren't friends on Mesa. Ask whoever sent it.

## Saved spots and your profile

### Where are my saved spots?

Profile → Spots, grouped by city. The map button shows them all as pins. Tap the heart again on any spot to remove it.

### How do I change my name, handle or photo?

Profile → Edit profile. Handles are 3 to 30 characters: letters, numbers, dots and underscores.

### Can I use dark mode?

Yes. Profile → Settings → Appearance. "System" follows your phone.

## Delete your account

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, photo, preferences, saved spots, crawls, lists, friends and stats, and links to anything you shared stop working. It can't be undone.

Can't get into the app? Email [support@mesamesa.app](mailto:support@mesamesa.app) from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm when it's done.
