# Mesa Privacy Policy

_Effective date: November 11, 2026_

Mesa ("we", "us") helps you find food and drink spots and plan crawls. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

| Data | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Account details | Email, name, handle, home city, profile photo (if you add one) | Sign-in and your profile |
| Sign-in data | A user ID from Apple or Google if you sign in with them | Sign-in |
| Location | Your device's location while you use the app, or a city you pick | Finding spots near you and planning crawls |
| Taste preferences | Vibes, budget, cuisines, walk/drive limits, plus anything you type in your own words | Personalizing results |
| Allergies & diet (optional) | Allergies you pick or type (e.g. shellfish, mushrooms) and diets (vegetarian, vegan, halal, kosher) | Steering suggestions and dishes away from things you can't eat |
| Activity | Spots and crawls you save, crawls you complete, distance walked on crawls | Your profile, stats and better picks |
| Browsing signals | Which spots you open and which you swipe away in Wildcard (how often and when last, not where you were) | Better picks; kept 90 days |
| Searches and crawl requests | What you type into search or "Anything else?" | Answering the request |
| Usage counts | How many requests your account made each day (a number, not what you did) | Knowing how many people use Mesa; kept 90 days |

We do **not** collect contacts, payment information or advertising identifiers, and we collect no health information other than the allergies and diet you choose to add. We do not track you across other apps or websites, show ads, or sell your data.

## Allergies and diet

Adding allergies or a diet is optional. Mesa uses them only to push suggestions and "Crowd favorites" dishes away from obvious conflicts. **Mesa can't guarantee any place or dish is allergen-free**, and you should always confirm with the restaurant. You can remove them anytime in your taste preferences; deleting your account deletes them.

## How location is used

Mesa asks for location only **while you use the app**. Your location is sent to our server with each request to find nearby places and is used to answer that request. We don't keep a history of where you've been. Spots and crawls you save are stored with their addresses and route.

## What stays on your phone

Your recent searches, the last city you picked by hand, your light or dark mode setting and your AI picks choice are stored on your device, not in your account. Deleting the app removes them.

## Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers that run Mesa for us:

- **Supabase** stores your account, profile, photo, preferences and saved items.
- **Amazon Web Services** hosts our server.
- **Google Maps Platform (Places API)**: we send search areas and search terms to find places. Place details, photos and reviews come from Google.
- **Google Maps (in the app)**: the maps for crawl routes and saved spots are drawn by Google's map software on your phone, which talks to Google directly under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Anthropic (Claude AI)**, only if you turn on **AI picks**. It's off until you say yes; Mesa asks once after you sign in. With it on, we send your taste preferences (including allergies and diet), a summary of the kinds of spots you save (counts by type and price, not the places themselves), your searches and crawl requests, the mood you pick, the time of day and day of the week, and the nearby places being ranked with the walking and driving time to each. We never send your name, email, photo, GPS coordinates or which spots you opened or swiped away. You can turn AI picks off anytime in Account settings. With it off, Mesa ranks with its own scoring and sends none of this to the AI provider. Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs from its systems within 30 days, except content it flags for violating its usage policy or must keep by law, and doesn't use them to train its models.
- **Anthropic and Google Gemini (AI), public place data only**: to summarize reviews and find press coverage of a place, we send its name, address and public reviews. Nothing about you is included.
- **Apple / Google sign-in**, if you choose them.

We may also share data if the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Beyond that:

- Crawls you plan but don't save or finish are deleted after 7 days.
- Browsing signals and daily usage counts are deleted after 90 days.
- Server logs (no request contents) are kept for up to 3 days.
- Totals that aren't tied to any person, such as how many searches Mesa ran in a day, are kept for up to 400 days.

## Your choices

- **Delete your account** in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, photo, preferences, saved spots, crawls and stats. If you can't get into the app, see [how to delete your account](/support/#delete-your-account).
- **Change or remove** your profile details and photo in Settings.
- **AI picks**: turn it on or off in Settings.
- **Location**: turn it off in your phone's settings and pick a city by hand instead.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data. Contact us to use them.

## Children

Mesa isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

We'll update this page and its effective date when this policy changes, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
