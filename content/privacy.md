# Mesa Privacy Policy

_Effective date: November 13, 2026_

Mesa ("we", "us"), based in California, helps you find food and drink spots and plan crawls. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- We collect what Mesa needs to work: your account, your taste, where you are while you use the app, and what you save.
- We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
- AI picks are off until you turn them on. With them on, your preferences, a summary of what you save and your searches go to Anthropic. Your name, email, photo and GPS coordinates never do.
- Location is used only while the app is open, and we don't keep a history of where you've been.
- Nothing you make is shared with other people until you choose to share it. Friends see your name, handle and photo, and nothing about friends or sharing goes to AI.
- You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app.

The rest of this page is the detail behind each of those.

## What Mesa asks your phone for

Mesa asks for two permissions, and works without either.

- **Location, while using the app.** Used to find spots near you and to fill in your home city if you tap "Use my current location". Say no and you can pick a city by hand.
- **Photo library.** Used only when you choose a profile picture. Mesa sees the one photo you pick, not your library. It never asks for the camera.

Mesa doesn't ask for contacts, the microphone, Bluetooth, notifications or tracking permission.

## How location is used

Mesa asks for location only **while you use the app**. Your location is sent to our server with each request to find nearby places and is used to answer that request. We don't keep a history of where you've been.

A few things do carry a place with them:

- Spots you save are stored with their address.
- Crawls you plan are stored with their starting point, stops and route, so you can open them again.
- Your home city, if you add one to your profile, is stored as text.

When you share a crawl, other people see its stops and route but not where it starts, unless you turn on "Show where it starts". Your home city and your current location are never shown to other people.

When you type a neighborhood or city into the location picker, what you type is sent to Google to suggest matches.

## Your profile photo

If you add a photo, it's shrunk on your phone and stored with our database provider. It's saved at a web address that anyone holding the link can open, which is how the app shows it to you and to the people described under [Friends and sharing](#friends-and-sharing). Removing the photo, or deleting your account, deletes it.

## Friends and sharing

Mesa has no public profiles and no public feed. Other people see something of yours only in these cases:

- **Your name, handle and photo** are shown to someone who looks up your exact handle, to your friends, to people who open a crawl or list you shared or your profile link, and to people on a list you edit together. Your email, home city, taste, allergies, location and which spots you saved are never shown to anyone.
- **Your profile counts** (how many spots you've saved, in how many cities, how many crawls you've finished, and how many friends you have) show on your page to anyone signed in to Mesa who reaches it. Only the numbers, never the spots or the friends themselves.
- **Your profile link** (Profile → Share profile) opens your page in the app for anyone signed in to Mesa. Opened without the app, the web page only says to get Mesa and shows nothing about you.
- **Friends** are people whose request you accepted or who accepted yours, and people who joined with one of your invite codes (or whose code you used). Either of you can end it at any time.
- **Crawls and lists are private** until you change that. "Friends" lets your friends see one. "Anyone with the link" also lets anyone you send the link to see it, including on a web page at mesamesa.app that shows the title, your name, and the names and addresses of the places. That page has no account behind it, so treat the link like the thing itself: anyone it's forwarded to can open it.
- **A shared crawl** shows its stops, route and times, plus the title, description and notes if you wrote any. It doesn't show where you started unless you turn that on, what you typed when planning it, or whether you finished it. If someone taps "I'm in", you see their name; other viewers see it only if they're friends with that person.
- **A shared list** shows its places and, to you and the friends who edit it, who added each one.
- **Blocking** someone hides you from each other everywhere in Mesa and ends the friendship. They aren't told. **Reports** go to us, and we don't tell the other person who sent one.

Shares hold only which places are in them. The names, photos and hours you see come fresh from Google each time one is opened.

Nothing in this section is sent to Anthropic or any other AI provider: not your friends, your lists, what you share, or what others share with you. The same goes for crawls you make yourself: their titles, descriptions and notes never go to AI.

## Allergies and diet

Adding allergies or a diet is optional. Mesa uses them only to push suggestions and "Crowd favorites" dishes away from obvious conflicts. **Mesa can't guarantee any place or dish is allergen-free**, and you should always confirm with the restaurant. You can remove them anytime in your taste preferences; deleting your account deletes them.

## AI picks

Mesa can use AI from Anthropic (Claude) to rank spots for you and plan crawls. It's off until you say yes: Mesa asks once after you sign in, and you can change your answer anytime in Profile → Settings → AI picks.

With AI picks **on**, we send Anthropic:

- your taste preferences, including any allergies and diet and anything you typed in your own words;
- a summary of the kinds of spots you save: counts by type, vibe and price, not the places themselves;
- what you type into search and into a crawl's "Anything else?" box;
- the mood you pick, the part of the day and the day of the week;
- the nearby places being ranked, with the walking and driving time to each.

We **never** send your name, email, handle, photo, GPS coordinates, which spots you opened or swiped away, or anything about your friends, lists or what you share.

With AI picks **off**, Mesa ranks with its own scoring and sends none of the above to Anthropic. The app keeps working; picks are just less tailored.

Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs from its systems within 30 days, except content it flags for violating its usage policy or must keep by law, and doesn't use them to train its models.

### AI that isn't about you

Separately from AI picks, Mesa uses Anthropic and Google Gemini to summarize a place's public reviews and to look for press coverage of it. For this we send the place's name, address and public reviews. Nothing about you is included, so this runs whether AI picks is on or off.

## What stays on your phone

Your recent searches, the last city you picked by hand, your light or dark mode setting and your AI picks choice are stored on your device, not in your account. Deleting the app removes them.

## This website

mesamesa.app sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ads. It loads its font from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address and browser, as with any site that uses it. If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your browser.

## Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers that run Mesa for us:

- **Supabase** stores your account, profile, photo, preferences, saved items, friends, lists and reports.
- **Amazon Web Services** hosts our server.
- **Google Maps Platform (Places API)**: we send search areas and search terms to find places, and what you type into the location picker to suggest cities. Place details, photos and reviews come from Google.
- **Google Maps (in the app)**: the maps for crawl routes and saved spots are drawn by Google's map software on your phone, which talks to Google directly under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Anthropic (Claude AI)**, for AI picks, only if you turn them on. See [AI picks](#ai-picks) for exactly what is sent.
- **Anthropic and Google Gemini (AI), public place data only**, as described above.
- **Apple / Google sign-in**, if you choose them.

Some things leave Mesa only because you ask them to: tapping "Take me there" opens your maps app with the spot's location, sharing a spot, crawl, list or invite code goes through your phone's share sheet, and press links open in your browser.

Other people on Mesa see what you share with them, as described under [Friends and sharing](#friends-and-sharing).

We may also share data if the law requires it.

## Where your data is stored

Supabase and our server are both in the United States (AWS us-east-1, Virginia). If you use Mesa from outside the US, your data is sent to and stored there.

Data travels between the app, our server and these providers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No system is perfectly secure, so we also keep what we hold to a minimum.

## How long we keep it

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Account, profile, photo, preferences, invite status, saved spots, saved and completed crawls | Until you remove them or delete your account |
| Friends, blocks, lists, and who your crawls and lists are shared with | Until you remove them or delete your account |
| Friend requests nobody answered | 90 days |

## Your choices

- **Delete your account** in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, photo, preferences, saved spots, crawls, lists, friends, stats and browsing signals. Links to things you shared stop working, and your unused invite codes are turned off. If you can't get into the app, see [how to delete your account](/support/#delete-your-account).
- **Change or remove** your profile details and photo in Profile → Edit profile.
- **Change your taste**, including allergies and diet, anytime. "Clear all" wipes it.
- **AI picks**: turn it on or off in Settings.
- **Location**: turn it off in your phone's settings and pick a city by hand instead.
- **Unsave** any spot or crawl to remove it from your account.
- **Sharing**: set any crawl or list back to Private, remove a friend, or block someone, anytime. Blocked people are listed in Settings → Blocked people.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to how it's used. Email us to use them. We won't treat you any differently for asking.

**If you live in California:** we don't sell or share your personal information, as California law defines those words, and we don't use it for targeted ads. Mesa doesn't track you across other apps and websites, so it behaves the same whether your browser or phone sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal or not.

## Children

Mesa is only for people 18 and older, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think someone under 18 has made an account, email us and we'll delete it and its data.
